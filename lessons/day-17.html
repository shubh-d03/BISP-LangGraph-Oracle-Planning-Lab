The big idea

A multi-step workflow makes one operation's result available to the next decision. The example loads units, loads prices and then calculates revenue. Keep each node small and carry the shared Scenario and Period through state. This lesson uses completed mock outcomes so you can understand the chain before adding real job polling.

Open audio file

Follow the workflow

In the success case, the units node records its result. The prices node appends another result without erasing the first. The revenue calculation follows after the required mock inputs have succeeded. Compare the failed-price-load case. Its evidence must remain in state, and the dependent calculation must be recorded as skipped. For real Oracle jobs, a submission identifier alone is not a completed prerequisite. The next lesson introduces the monitoring needed at that boundary.

Open audio file

If you are stuck

If you are stuck, inspect the accumulated results after every node. If only the latest result remains, you overwrote the history instead of appending to it. Keep each job identifier attached to its own operation. Use the same Planning point of view across the chain rather than hard-coding different values in different nodes. Test a controlled failure with fake functions and prove that the dependent calculation was not called.
